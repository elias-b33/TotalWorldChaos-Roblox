The Roblox games we compete with, what each does well and badly, and where Total World Chaos fits, for both the PvP match and the singleplayer sandbox. Numbers are tracker snapshots from 28 October 2026 (concurrent players, CCU, change by the hour; visits and like ratios move slowly). Sources are at the end.

## 2. What the market tells us

1. **The niche is real and big:** about 45,000–55,000 players are in a nation-strategy game on Roblox at any moment. Two games hold most of them.
2. **Two designs dominate, each with a hole:**
   - *OpenFront land grabs* (RoFront, Pixel Conquest, Pixel Front, likely WorldFront) are quick and readable but shallow. They're on real maps, players can be knocked out within two minutes, and they're easy to clone, so four already exist.
   - *Grand strategy* (Conquer The World WW2, Rise of Nations, Control Europe) is deep but slow and complex for phones. Conquer The World sells 39 game passes, and players criticise passes like double research and focus speed as pay to win. Players also report lag on mobile.
3. **Every top rival uses the real world or WWII.** Nobody offers fantasy races or sci-fi peoples on their own maps.
4. **Fairness and naval play get liked:** Navy War (97%) and Control Europe (94%) beat the leaders' 81–83%.
5. **WorldBox-style sandboxes have an audience on Roblox:** World Sim reached 5,000 players two months after release with a 91% like ratio, then faded to a few hundred. Its pull is the sandbox; its drop suggests it lacks a reason to come back.

## 3. Rival by rival: what to learn, where we win

| Rival | Learn from it | Where we win |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RoFront | Instant readability (colour = territory), big busy servers, quick rounds, flags and skins in a locker | Players knocked out early come back as Primordials instead of sitting out; races and three distinct maps; real diplomacy (alliances, insults, gifts, events) instead of one alliance button; phone-first sheets |
| Conquer The World WW2 | Depth keeps players for months; formables (our "Form an empire", planned) | No pay to win, as a selling point against its 39 passes; 25-minute matches that fit a phone session; runs smoothly on phones |
| Control Europe | A 94% like ratio from a focused, fair design; saved maps in private servers | Three maps and peoples instead of one region; our sandbox saves your own world |
| WorldFront | Naval invasions and late-game spectacle | Navies with sea zones and transports, and space fleets on Skyshards; the Cataclysm phase as the late-game spectacle |
| Pixel Conquest, Pixel Front | Proof that the OpenFront loop sells | A loop that isn't a clone: provinces, sieges, troop types and counters, phases |
| Rise of Nations | Long-term nation building is loved (313M visits) | The same fantasy in 25 minutes with a fresh start each match |
| Navy War | Ships make people happy (97%) | Fleets are part of a bigger war, not the whole game |
| Country War RTS, Build and Conquer | Simple real-time control | The Slate & Brass GUI and pixel-art map look premium next to them |
| World Sim (sandbox) | Pure creation and destruction draws younger players fast | Our sandbox shares its map, peoples and powers with a live PvP game, so a sandbox player always has a next step: play online |

## 4. How we take our share

**The line for the store page:** *Fantasy world conquest for phones. Ten nations, orcs, elves, robots and humans, 25-minute wars, fleets and space fleets. Get knocked out? Come back as a Primordial and rain meteors. No pay to win.*

**Two front doors into one game:**

1. **PvP matches** for the RoFront and Conquer The World crowd who want something fresh. Lead with the fantasy map (orcs against elves), Primordial meteors and fleets. That's the thumbnail; every rival's is a world or WWII map.
2. **The sandbox** for WorldBox fans and younger players: "your own world" in the start menu. Fill the thumbnail rotation with sandbox shots too, so World Sim's audience finds us. Every sandbox session ends one tap from Play online.

**What to build or stress, in order (most already planned):**

1. Launch with **all three maps**: the fantasy and sci-fi maps are the difference, so they must look their best (the flag and banner art pass belongs here).
2. **No pay to win** in the store description and the shop footer (already in the shop).
3. **Bots that feel like players** (the bot strategy stage), so a 10-nation match works even at low player counts, which no OpenFront clone handles as well.
4. **Short sessions done well:** 25-minute default, Blitz (about 12 minutes) for a quick game, Long for the grand-strategy crowd (planned modes).
5. **The sandbox as a retention tool, not a dead end:** saved worlds, sandbox quests that teach the PvP game, and the optional Sandbox Pass (`docs/MONETIZATION.md` 13.2). World Sim's fade shows a pure sandbox needs a reason to come back. Ours has the PvP match and the season pass.
6. **Content cadence:** weekly twists and seasons (planned), because the leaders update every few days.

**A realistic share:** 5–10% of the niche, about 2,500–5,000 players at peak, plus the sandbox's own audience, puts us in the top four. The `docs/MONETIZATION.md` base case (2,000 peak) is conservative against that. 15,000 needs a breakout: a creator video, or the Roblox front page through strong retention.

## 5. Watch list

- **OpenFront clones keep multiplying**, so readability and match quality are what separate us.
- **A fantasy OpenFront clone** could appear any week. Our depth (races, navies, diplomacy, Primordials) and polish are the moat.
- **The WorldBox Project's sandbox** shares our races (humans, orcs, elves, dwarves). Track its numbers after launch.
