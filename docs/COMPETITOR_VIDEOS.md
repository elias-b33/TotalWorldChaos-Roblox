## 2. What the videos tell us

1. **Creators cover new strategy hits within two weeks.** A cluster of guide channels (CHALLS/ItsChalls, NoobBlox, TonyCool, ItzVexo, Radex Tips) made RoFront guides in its first fortnight. They will cover us too if we get any traction; give them something to explain (races, counters, Primordials, navies).
2. **Stories beat tutorials, and the stories are always the same few:** a famous empire or leader conquering everything; the tiniest or hardest nation winning; one player against the whole server; from nothing to world domination. Our game must produce those stories on its own.
3. **Spectacle gets its own videos:** nukes, missiles, forts. Our equivalents are Primordial meteors, the Cataclysm, land scars, sea invasions and beasts.
4. **Updates, formables and hosted events keep old games on YouTube** (Rise of Nations, six years old, still gets weekly videos).
5. **Timelapses are the sandbox's viral engine:** "1,000 years of history" videos have 0.3–3.5 million views each. A sandbox that runs history and shows it well gets these videos for free.
6. **Cheaters target land-grab games early** (an auto-play script for WorldFront). Our server-authoritative rules and bots sending the same commands are the right defence; keep the CommandRouter's checks and rate limits strict.
